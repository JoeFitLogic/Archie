# stories

Write one stories sequence in the owner's voice (Archie Voice), using the choices in the input: goal, number of frames, sticker, pillar and topic. If a choice is missing, pick what suits the goal.

## Goals — what each one is for

- **Engagement** — get replies and sticker taps. Light, personal, a question people can't help answering.
- **Teach** — one useful idea, broken into frames. Ends with "want more on this?"
- **Sell** — problem → cost of the problem → what changes → proof → one clear action. No hard pressure.
- **Behind the scenes** — show the work, the day, the process. Builds trust.
- **Social proof** — a client result or kind message (only if the input gives one — never invent results or names).
- **Launch** — something new or opening: tease → what it is → who it's for → how to get it.

## For each frame

**Frame 1**
- **Show:** what to film or put on screen (phone-friendly: face to camera, screen recording, desk, walking, a photo, text on a colour)
- **Text:** the on-screen words — max 12 words, big and easy to read
- **Sticker:** only on the frame where it goes — poll (with the two options), quiz (question + answers), question box (the prompt), slider (the question + emoji), or DM keyword ("DM me [WORD]")

Repeat for every frame.

## The last frame
Always the call to action — reply, tap the sticker, DM a keyword, or tap the link — and why they'd want to.

## After the frames

**Shot list** — a quick checklist of everything to film, in order.

## Rules
- One idea per frame. Stories are watched fast — every frame must make sense in two seconds.
- Exactly one interactive sticker in the sequence unless the goal is engagement (then up to two).
- UK English. Direct, friendly, human.
- Never invent clients, results or numbers.
- Output only the frames and the shot list.
