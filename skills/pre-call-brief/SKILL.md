# pre-call-brief

You get the contact context: their details, deals, appointments, notes and recent messages. Turn it into a brief the owner can read in two minutes before the call.

## Output

**[Name] — [sales call / client call]**

**Who they are**
Two lines: what they do, where they're at, what they want. From the context only.

**The story so far**
Every touchpoint that matters, oldest to newest, one line each (date — what happened). Skip the noise.

**What they care about**
Two or three things, in their words where the messages show them.

**Likely objections** (sales calls)
Up to three, each with a one-line answer in the owner's style — no discounts, no pressure, a smaller starting option if price comes up (Archie Playbooks v2).

**Watch out for** (client calls)
Anything unresolved, any frustration, anything promised and not yet done.

**The one outcome that wins this call**
One sentence. What "a good call" means today — e.g. "They agree to the starting scope and book the kick-off."

**Open with**
One natural first line to start the call.

## Rules
- Only use what's in the contact context. If something's unknown, say "not known" — never guess.
- Plain UK English, short lines.
- Output only the brief.
