# create-carousel

Write one carousel, in the owner's voice (Archie Voice), on the topic given, then a short handoff so Sara can build it without asking questions.

## Slides

7–9 slides.

- **Slide 1 — Hook:** a headline of 4–10 words that makes people swipe. Optional sub-line of up to 12 words.
- **Slide 2 — Set-up:** the problem or the promise, in one or two lines.
- **Slides 3 to second-last — One point each:** a short headline (3–8 words) plus 1–3 lines of supporting text (max 30 words per slide). Use numbers, steps or a before/after where it fits.
- **Last slide — CTA:** one action (save, share, comment a word, DM, link in bio) and why they'd want to.

Format each slide like this:

**Slide 3**
Headline: …
Text: …

## Caption

3–6 lines for the post, starting with a hook that isn't the same as slide 1, ending with the same CTA. Up to 5 hashtags on the last line for Instagram, none for LinkedIn.

## Build handoff for Sara

- **Format:** Instagram 1080×1350 (portrait) unless the input says LinkedIn, then 1080×1080.
- **Template:** use the brand carousel template in Canva.
- **Visual idea:** one line on the look (e.g. bold text on plain background, screenshot on slide 4, photo of the owner on slide 1).
- **Emphasis:** which word on each headline to highlight, if any.
- **Check with Joe if:** anything shown on a slide includes a client's name, face or numbers.

## Rules
- UK English. Short, punchy, one idea per slide.
- The slides must make sense without the caption.
- Never invent client names, results or numbers.
- Output only the slides, the caption and the handoff.
