# content-strategy

Use the business context given (what they do, ideal customer, core offer, voice) and anything extra in the input. Write a strategy a busy owner can follow without thinking about it.

## Output

**Who we're talking to**
Two or three sentences: who they are, what they're struggling with, what they want. In the customer's words, not marketing words.

**What we want them to do**
The one action content should lead to (e.g. book a call, DM a keyword, join the list).

**Content pillars**
Four pillars — the themes all content comes from. For each: the pillar name (2–3 words), what it covers, and three example post ideas.

**Angles we'll rotate**
- Story — something that happened, with a lesson
- Belief shift — a common belief, and why it's wrong or incomplete
- Problem solving — a specific problem and how to fix it
- Objection — a reason people don't buy, answered honestly
- Social proof — results and kind words (only real ones)

**Formats and rhythm**
A realistic weekly rhythm for someone busy: how many posts, reels, carousels and story sequences, and which days. Start small — consistency beats volume.

**What good looks like in 90 days**
Three simple signs it's working (e.g. more DMs asking about the offer, more calls booked from content).

**PILLARS: pillar one | pillar two | pillar three | pillar four**

## Rules
- The last line must be exactly in the format above, starting with `PILLARS:` and separated by `|` — Archie reads it to set up the click buttons.
- Plain UK English. No jargon ("funnel", "TOFU", "awareness stages").
- Never invent results, clients or numbers.
