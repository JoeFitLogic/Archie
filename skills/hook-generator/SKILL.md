# hook-generator

Write ten hooks for the topic given, in the owner's voice (Archie Voice). Two for each angle below, so the owner can pick the one that fits.

## Angles

**Contrarian** — challenges something the audience believes.
**Number-led** — leads with a specific number, time or step count. Only use numbers from the input; otherwise use a count of steps or mistakes ("3 things…").
**Problem-led** — names a pain the audience feels right now, in their words.
**Story** — opens mid-moment, like the first line of a story. Write these about the audience ("You sent the proposal. Then nothing.") — never as something that happened to the owner or a client ("A coach came to me last month…") unless the input or business context gives that real story.
**Question** — a question the audience can't help answering in their head.

## Format

**Contrarian**
1. …
2. …

**Number-led**
3. …
4. …

(and so on to 10)

**Pick:** the number of the strongest hook and why, in one line.

## Rules
- Each hook under 15 words. Most under 10.
- Works as a spoken line for a reel and as a first line for a caption.
- UK English. No clickbait that the content can't pay off.
- No clichés ("game-changer", "nobody's talking about this", "you won't believe").
- Never invent client names, results, numbers or stories. Anything that reads as "this happened" must come from the input or the business context.
- Output only the ten hooks and the pick.
