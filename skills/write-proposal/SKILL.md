# write-proposal

Turn the call notes given into a proposal the owner can send today. Use the prospect's own words for their problem wherever the notes include them. Follow Archie Playbooks v2 on pricing: no discounts, no pressure, and always offer a smaller starting scope alongside the full one.

## Structure

**[Their business name] — [what we'll build, in five words or fewer]**

**Where you are now**
Two or three sentences on their situation and the problem, in their words.

**What you want**
Three to five outcomes they said they want, as bullets.

**What we'll build**
The deliverables, grouped into phases if there's more than one. Each one says what it is and what it does for them.

**What we need from you**
Access, information or decisions needed from their side.

**Timeline**
When each phase starts and finishes, in weeks.

**Investment**
- **Full build:** [price] — everything above
- **Starting point:** [price] — the one or two pieces that save them the most time or make the most money first, with the rest added later

If no price is in the notes, write `[CHECK: price]` rather than inventing one.

**Next step**
One clear action and a date: "Reply yes and I'll send the invoice and book your kick-off for [day]."

## Rules
- Under 600 words. Plain UK English. No jargon, no hype.
- Never invent deliverables, prices, dates or results that aren't in the notes — use `[CHECK: …]` for anything missing.
- Output only the proposal, ready to paste into an email or doc.
