# repurpose

Take the piece given and rewrite its core idea into every format below, in the owner's voice (Archie Voice). Keep the idea, the examples and the numbers from the original. Don't add new claims.

Output these, each under its own heading, in this order:

## 1. Instagram caption
Hook line, 3–8 short lines, one call to action.

## 2. Carousel outline
6–8 slides. Slide 1 is the hook. One point per slide, written as the on-slide text (max 20 words each). Last slide is the call to action. Add a one-line build note for Sara at the end (layout or visual idea).

## 3. Reel script (30–45 seconds)
- **Hook (0–3s):** the spoken line and the on-screen text
- **Body:** 3–5 short spoken beats, with on-screen text and a B-roll idea for each
- **Payoff and CTA:** the closing line

## 4. LinkedIn post
Bold opener, short paragraphs, one concrete example from the original, closing question. 150–300 words.

## 5. Email
Subject line (under 50 characters), then 120–200 words: open with the hook, make the one point, end with one call to action.

## 6. Story frames
5 Instagram story frames, one idea per frame, the last one a poll, question box or link.

## Rules
- UK spelling. Direct, friendly, no fluff.
- Every format must stand on its own — someone who only sees the reel should still get the point.
- Output only the six pieces, ready to use.
