# Archie Content Frameworks

## Awareness stages (Schwartz)

Every piece targets one stage. Pick it before writing.

- **Unaware** — they don't know they have the problem. Lead with a story or a surprising observation. Never mention the product.
- **Problem aware** — they feel the pain but haven't named it. Name it. Agitate briefly. No solution yet.
- **Solution aware** — they know solutions exist but not which. Compare approaches. Position the category.
- **Product aware** — they know the product. Proof, specifics, objection handling.
- **Most aware** — ready to buy. Offer, urgency, CTA.

Most content should sit at problem or solution aware. Product and most aware only when the brief says so.

## GAP axes

Every piece angles from one of three:

- **Goal** — what they want. "Imagine your Monday starting with the numbers already done."
- **Avoidance** — what they want to escape. "Stop finding out a client left three weeks after they did."
- **Problem** — what's actually broken. "Your CRM knows who's gone quiet. Nobody's looking."

## Carousel structure

7 to 9 slides. Every slide under 25 words.

1. **Hook slide** — one line, big claim or question. No context.
2. **Reframe** — why the obvious take is wrong.
3–6. **Body** — one idea per slide. Numbered if it's a list, narrative if it's a story.
7. **The turn** — the insight that changes how they see it.
8. **CTA slide** — one action. Save, comment a word, DM, link in bio.

Slides are read in two seconds each. If a slide needs re-reading, cut it.

## Reel structure

30 to 45 seconds. Written as a script with on-screen text markers.

- **Hook (0–3s)** — the first line decides everything. Pattern interrupt, bold claim, or specific number.
- **Setup (3–8s)** — the situation. One sentence.
- **Body (8–38s)** — the point, delivered in three beats maximum.
- **CTA (38–45s)** — one action.

Mark on-screen text with `[TEXT: ...]`. Mark cuts with `[CUT]`. Write for speaking, not reading — contractions, fragments, rhythm.

## Email structure

- **Subject line** — under 8 words. Three variants: curiosity, direct, specific-number.
- **Preview text** — extends the subject, doesn't repeat it. Under 90 characters.
- **Opening line** — no "hope you're well". First sentence earns the second.
- **Body** — 100 to 250 words. One idea. Short paragraphs, mostly one or two sentences.
- **CTA** — one link, one action. Repeated once at the end.
- **Sign-off** — first name only.

## Caption structure (carousel and reel)

- **First line** — stands alone, under 125 characters, because that's all that shows before "more".
- **Body** — 3 to 8 lines expanding the hook. Line breaks between thoughts.
- **CTA** — one action.
- **Hashtags** — up to 5, after a line break (or none if it reads better without). Mix of niche and broad.
